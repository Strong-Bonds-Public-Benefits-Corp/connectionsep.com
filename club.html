The Sanctuary Club

Founding membership in the place we're building — a community that knows your name, not a customer list.

Why cohorts, not year-round.

We open enrollment in cohorts on purpose. A founding community that joins together actually knows each other — and the commitment runs both ways: members stay through the hard months, and the anonymous trends that feed the Resilience Heat Map stay meaningful because they follow a steady community over time. When enrollment closes, it stays closed until the next cohort. That's not a marketing trick. It's how a community forms.

Founding enrollment runs four weeks: two weeks before our doors open, and two weeks after. Then it closes. A second cohort opens later, when the roastery opens.

The journey: from first sip to pillar.

Every member walks the same anchor track. You start with a welcome badge, and you grow from there.

  1. First Sip Welcome badge — every member starts here. You're in. You belong here.
  2. Safe Harbor · Level 1 You found the place. The lighthouse level — a safe place to land.
  3. Community Link · Level 2 You know people here. You're connected, not just attending.
  4. Growth Seeker · Level 3 You're growing. You're building something in yourself, not just getting by.
  5. Pillar of Support · Level 4 You hold the door for others. The people who came after you find their footing because you did.

Track names, levels, and colors are decided. The one-line descriptions are draft wording for discussion.

Tiers.

Membership is annual, paid upfront — that's the commitment the founding cohort is built on. (The one exception is the student plan below, which splits the same total across three months.)

Full integration — holding space for others.

$100 / year — 1 person

The Ally Option (Connection Partner): the 2-person price on each tier above. Pairs two members at a reduced combined rate — a spouse, a battle buddy, a friend — combating social isolation through mutual accountability and peer support.

Student plan

The $75 tier as $25 a month for three months — the same $75 total, so a student doesn't have to front it all at once to join.

Gift a tier — 10% off

A parent can buy a membership for a college student. A platoon sergeant can buy one for an isolated soldier in the barracks. The gift is for someone who needs the place.

What your membership does.

Your anonymous check-ins feed the Resilience Heat Map — the collective picture leaders see, so support can reach people before crisis, not after. Nobody's individual check-in is ever shared. Founding members don't just join a club. They build the place.
