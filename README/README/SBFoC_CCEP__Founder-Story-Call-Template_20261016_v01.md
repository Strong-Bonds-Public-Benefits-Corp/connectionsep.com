# Founder Story Call Template
**For:** Tony's calls with Dawn and Vic to fill in their Our Story sections
**Date created:** 2026-10-16

## How to use
Call them. Don't text the questions — you'll get better answers hearing their voice. Take notes in their words, not yours. Read back what you wrote and ask "did I get that right?"

## The questions

### 1. Your quote (the card)
"In one or two lines, in your own voice — if a stranger reads only this, what do they know about you?"
_This goes on the homepage card and the top of your Our Story section._

### 2. Your story (the page)
"In three to five sentences — what brought you to this mission? Raw and real, like you'd tell a friend."
_This goes on the Our Story page under your quote._

### 3. Your role line
"How do you want to be titled? Not your Army rank — your role here."
_Example: "Family Systems Lead. The Village Navigator."_

### 4. Anything off-limits?
"Is there anything I should NOT put on the website? Anything that's yours to tell in person, not in pixels?"

## After the call
- Send them what you wrote. Nothing goes live without their blessing.
- Marylou's rule applies to everyone: their words, their sign-off.
