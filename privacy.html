Privacy

How we protect you.

We built this site for people who need a safe place. That includes how we treat your information. This page says plainly what we collect, what we never collect, and what we never do with it.

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

Right now, the check-in is a demonstration.

The Resilience Check In on this site doesn't send or store anything yet. You can fill in the form, move the slider, and see the completion screen — nothing leaves your device. When the live system launches, this page will be updated to describe exactly what it stores, before it stores anything.

What the live check-in will collect — and why.

When the live system launches, the check-in will ask for:

  • Zip code — the only location we ask for. It's what puts your check-in on the Resilience Heat Map. No address, no tracking — just your zip.
  • Birth year — shows Heat Map trends by age group, so leaders can see which ages need more support. Just the year — never your birthday.
  • Race / Ethnicity — shows whether we're actually reaching everyone in El Paso, not just some neighborhoods or some communities. Leaders can't fix gaps they can't see.
  • Military connection and Branch of service — shows how service members, veterans, and families are really doing, so support can reach people before crisis, not after. Resources aren't the same across branches, so this helps leaders see each branch clearly.
  • College — UTEP, EPCC, and our other campuses face different pressures. This shows leaders on each campus where their students actually stand.
  • Alias — only if you choose one. You can always stay anonymous.

What we never collect.

  • Your name — unless you give it for a store order (see below).
  • Your street address for the Heat Map — store orders ship to the zip you entered.
  • Your exact location — we don't use IP tracking or geolocation, on purpose.
  • Any link between your identity and your Heat Map dot — identity never reaches the map pipeline.

The Heat Map shows groups, never people.

The Resilience Heat Map shows averages, not individuals. Nothing appears for a zip area until at least 10 check-ins land there, and nothing appears for a filtered view until at least 5. Below that, the area shows grey — “not enough data yet.” Never a zero, never one person's score.

Store orders.

When the Resilience Market opens for orders, checkout runs through Square. Your payment details go to Square, not to us. Anything you enter below the privacy line on the check-in form is used only for your order — never for the Heat Map, and it will never be shared, sold, or given away.

Survivor Journal stories.

When the Survivor Journal opens, stories sent for it are reviewed before anything appears. You can post anonymously or under a first name or alias. Nothing publishes without review, and you can ask for your story to be removed at any time.

Contact.

If you write to us at one of the addresses on the Contact page, your message goes to that inbox. We don't ask for sensitive details, and we don't share your message.

What we never do.

We never sell, share, or give away your information. We don't run advertising trackers on this site. These pages do load fonts and map tiles from outside services, which see the same basic request information any website visit generates — that's the extent of it.

If this page changes.

The date at the top changes with it. Questions about privacy: hello@connectionsep.com.
